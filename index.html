MoMo AI OS

A private, single-user personal organizer.

MoMo AI OS is a personal app used by one person to organize their own household records, projects and reading notes in their own Google Drive. It files documents, keeps an activity log, and maintains folders and spreadsheets in that one Google account.

MoMo AI OS is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not sell anything.
